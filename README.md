## How to Use

1. Sign up using your Gmail account and create your password.
2. After successful registration, sign in using the same email and password.
3. Once logged in, start adding your daily financial records:
   - Income (Pocket money, stipend, salary, etc.)
   - Expenses (Food, travel, shopping, subscriptions, etc.)
4. Track your spending habits through the dashboard.
5. Monitor your savings and manage your budget more effectively.

## Purpose

WealthWise is designed primarily for students and young professionals who want a simple way to manage and track their finances.

Many students receive a fixed monthly allowance but struggle to understand where their money goes. WealthWise helps users:

- Track daily expenses and income.
- Monitor savings in real time.
- Build better financial habits.
- Gain visibility into spending patterns.
- Make smarter budgeting decisions.

The goal is to provide an easy-to-use personal finance tracker that helps users stay financially aware and organized.
